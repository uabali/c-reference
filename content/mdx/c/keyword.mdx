<p>It is unspecified whether any of the spellings of these keywords, their alternate forms, or <code>true</code> and <code>false</code> is implemented as a predefined macro.</p>

<p>Each name that begins with a double underscore <code>__</code> or an underscore <code>_</code> followed by an uppercase letter is reserved: see <a href="/reference/c/language/identifier/#Reserved_identifiers">identifier</a> for details.</p>

<p>Note that digraphs <code>&lt;%</code>, <code>%&gt;</code>, <code>&lt;:</code>, <code>:&gt;</code>, <code>%:</code>, and <code>%:%:</code> provide an <a href="/reference/c/language/operator_alternative/">alternative way to represent standard tokens</a>.</p>
